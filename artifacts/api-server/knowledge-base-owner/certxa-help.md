## Settings (/settings) — sections and what each holds
Personal
- Personal Details /settings/personal-details — the owner's name, photo and contact info.
- My Preferences /settings/my-preferences — notification defaults and calendar sync: a calendar subscription link that Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook can show. It is one-way only (Certxa appointments appear in their calendar). There is no two-way sync: events in Google Calendar do not come into Certxa.
- Security /settings/security — the password they sign in with.
Team
- Staff & Roles → /team — team members, roles and permissions; adding staff (/team/add).
- Earnings & Commission /settings/commission — pay frequency, pay-period start day and commission rules.
- Payroll → /payroll — run payroll, review accruals and tax documents.
Business
- Business Details /settings/business — salon name, address, logo and contact details.
- Business Hours /settings/hours — weekly open and close times for each day.
- Booking Page /settings/online-booking — the public booking page, its widget and availability.
- Booking Controls /settings/booking-controls — approval, how much notice is needed, how far ahead clients can book, card on file or a deposit for online bookings, cancellation policy and fees.
- Client Notifications /settings/client-notifications — confirmations, reminders and review requests, by text, e-mail or both.
- Check-in & Kiosk /settings/kiosk — the check-in tablet's address, its QR code and welcome text.
- POS Stations /settings/registers — more than one checkout station, each with its own front desk tablet address.
- Cash Drawers /settings/cash-drawers — each cash drawer, its starting cash and the stations that use it.
- Language /settings/language — display language for staff screens.
- Advanced Features /settings/advanced — turn platform features on or off.
- Import & Export /settings/data-transfer — bring in clients, services, products, gift cards and appointments from another system (Vagaro, Square, Fresha, Booksy, GlossGenius, Mindbody, or a CSV/Excel file), or export.
Subscription
- Plan & Billing → /billing — the plan, usage, payment method and invoices; change plan or cancel.

## Checkout & Tax (/payments/checkout-tax)
Sales tax rate and what it applies to (services, add-ons, retail products, gift cards — tips are never taxed), quick area-code buttons for typing phone numbers, cash price (percent off for cash), dual screen mode, tip choices, and what the CARD button does (the Certxa card reader through Stripe, or the salon's own card machine).

## The front desk (/nail)
- Tabs: Queue, Ticket#, Check-In, More.
- The first time the owner opens it, Front Desk Setup walks through sales tax, card payments, cash price, the first cash drawer, tip choices, turn rotation, Tech IDs and the manager PIN. It can be opened again any time.
- More menu: Book Appointment, Client Lookup, Redeem Voucher, In / Out (time clock), Retail Sale, My Slip, Language, Manager.
- Manager menu (needs the manager PIN): Settings, Front Desk Setup, Cash Drawer, Messages, Key Grid (arrange the front desk keys), Turn Rotation (how turns are counted), Service Times (actual vs planned), Manager PIN (change it — every salon starts with 2026 until it's changed), Lock Screen (Tech ID to use the front desk, on or off), Reports.
- Staff sign in with their own Tech ID when the lock screen is on. Every team member, including the owner, gets a Tech ID automatically.
- The front desk screen works in English, Vietnamese, Spanish and French (More → Language).
- Day Close: it opens on its own on the front desk once the salon's closing time has passed and nothing is open or waiting; it counts the drawer and closes the day. There is no Day Close button to tap — it appears by itself.
- It keeps working if the internet goes down: tickets continue and cash can be taken; card payments wait for the internet.

## Things the assistant can do for the owner right here (after they tap Confirm)
Sales tax, the CARD button, cash price, tip choices, turn rotation, the Tech ID lock screen, cash drawers and their starting cash, and opening hours. For anything else, point to the page above.

## Things the assistant never does
Make, move or cancel bookings (use the Calendar or the front desk), take payments or refunds, change service prices, change the manager PIN or team members.
