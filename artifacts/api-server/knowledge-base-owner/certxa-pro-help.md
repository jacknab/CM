# Certxa Pro app — where things are (checked against the app's code 2026-11-03)

The Certxa Pro app is for independent beauty pros: one calendar (yours), your clients, card payments, and your
booking website. It has five tabs at the bottom: Home, Calendar, Clients, POS, More.

## Home
- Today at a glance: what you have taken today (paid visits, POS quick sales included), the next appointment, and
  today's appointments. Tap an appointment to open it.

## Calendar
- Month, Week and Day views (buttons at the top left). The red line in Day view is the time now.
- The button at the top right makes a new booking.
- Hold a booking to move it to another time (a paid booking can't be moved).
- Tap a booking to open it: Schedule (change the date and time), Text, Call, Email, Notes, CHECKOUT, MARK AS PAID,
  DELETE APPOINTMENT. Once it's paid: REBOOK and REFUND (full or part). A paid booking can't be edited, moved,
  cancelled or deleted.

## Clients
- Search by name, phone or e-mail. Filters: All, Upcoming, New, Regular, At risk, Inactive, VIP.
  New = visited recently, fewer than 3 visits. Regular = 3 or more visits. At risk = not back for 60 days or more.
  Inactive = never visited, or not back for 120 days or more. VIP = marked by you.
- The + button at the top right adds a client (name and phone).
- Tap a client for their profile: Text, Call and Email icons under the name (Text is grey for a landline, Email
  grey with no e-mail), Book appointment, visits, lifetime spend, average visit, last 12 months, last and next
  visit, no-shows, loyalty points, upcoming and past visits, notes (pin the important ones), details, tags, and the
  switches for marketing texts and review requests.
- The pencil at the top right of a profile edits name, phone, e-mail, "goes by", birthday and allergies.

## POS
- A quick sale: type the amount and what it's for, choose or add the client, then check out (Tap to Pay on iPhone or
  the Stripe M2 reader). Quick sales don't show on the calendar but count in what you've taken.

## More
- Accept Payments card: start taking card payments through Certxa (Stripe).
- Plus Plan card: $3 a month; notification texts (confirmations, reminders, receipts and similar) are free while
  Plus is on.
- Booking requests: approve, offer other times, or decline (when Booking approval is set to review each one).
- Reviews: what clients said, and reply.
- Credit balance: your text message credits and the packages to buy more.
- Business AI: this chat.
- Settings.

## Settings (More → Settings)
- MY BUSINESS: Business details (name, type, phone, e-mail), Location (address; sets your time zone), Where you see
  clients (your place, house calls, or both), Business hours (time zone; tick the days you're open and tap a day to
  set its times), Services (what you offer, how long it takes and the price), Catalog (categories, services, add-ons,
  packages, deals, products).
- ONLINE BOOKING: Booking rules (who can book, notice, start times, cancelling), Booking approval (accept bookings
  automatically, or review each one), Cancellation policy, Blocked clients (phone numbers that cannot book online).
- WEBSITE: Website — your booking link (yourname.certxa.com), share it, open it, or edit the link, design and look.
- ABOUT YOU: More → Settings → Website → About you — the text and your photo on the About tab of your website. Tap Write it with AI and Business AI asks a few questions, writes it, and puts it on your website when you tap Confirm.
- CLIENT MESSAGES: Client notifications (reminders, confirmations and review requests).
- PAYMENTS: Stripe account (where card payments are paid out), Enable Tap to Pay on iPhone, Connect card reader
  (Stripe M2), Deposits (ask clients for a deposit when they book), Sales tax (tax added at checkout).
- ACCOUNT: Account — your mobile number (a new one is confirmed by a texted code), change password (e-mailed link),
  sign out, delete your account.

## Text message credits
- Every new account starts with 50 free credits. Every text Certxa sends for you uses 1 credit, even if the
  phone company doesn't deliver it. With no credits left, texts are not sent (e-mails still go).
- With Plus on, notification texts are free; other texts (review requests, replies, win-back and marketing texts)
  still use a credit.
- Buy more in More → Credit balance.

## What the app does not have
- No team, staff, payroll, commission, turns or cash drawers: the app is one person's business.
- Tip choices are not in the app's Settings (Business AI can change them).
