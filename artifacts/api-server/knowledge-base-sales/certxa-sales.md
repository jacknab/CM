## What Certxa is
All-in-one software for salons: online booking, calendar, front desk checkout, check-in kiosk, walk-in line and waitlist, turn rotation, payroll and commissions, loyalty points, gift cards, automatic client texts, Google reviews, reports, a website builder, and Autumn, the AI phone receptionist.
Built for nail salons, hair salons, barbershops, spas, massage studios, hair removal studios, and braid and weave salons.
It runs in a web browser on a computer or tablet, and front desk tablets can run the Certxa Android app.

## Trial, contracts, billing
- Every salon plan starts with a 60-day free trial. No credit card is needed to start.
- No long-term contracts and no commissions on your sales. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel any time.
- Paying yearly costs ten months instead of twelve.
- Sign up at certxa.com (Tom can text the sign-up link).

## What changes between plans (the only enforced differences)
- How many staff members: shown in the live plan list. The owner does not count toward the limit.
- How many locations: one, except Enterprise (up to 3).
- How many Autumn calls are included each month: shown in the live plan list.
Every other feature on this sheet is part of every salon plan.

## Taking payments
- Cards run through Stripe: 2.7% + $0.05 in person, 2.9% + $0.30 online (Stripe's rate), plus a $0.60 Certxa fee per card transaction. Both apply to every card payment processed through Certxa. Stripe pays the money out to the salon's bank account.
- In person, cards are taken on the Stripe M2 card reader.
- A salon can keep its own card machine instead: the CARD button then just records the payment as card, and Certxa charges nothing on it (their own processor's fees still apply, from their processor).
- Cash, gift cards and split payments are all supported.
- Online bookings can require a card on file or a deposit (a percentage or a fixed amount), through Stripe.

## Online booking
- A booking page where clients pick the service, the technician and the time, from any phone or computer, any time.
- Nail salons can offer options like length, shape and design.
- Clients get text and e-mail confirmations and reminders, and can reschedule or cancel from their confirmation link.
- The booking link can go on the salon's website, its Google Business Profile and Instagram.

## Calendar
Live calendar for the whole team, color-coded by team member, drag and drop to move appointments, block time for breaks and days off. Walk-ins show alongside bookings.

## Front desk checkout
- Tickets with services, add-ons, discounts and tips, split payments, receipts, cash drawer, and a day close with a cash count.
- Works on several front desk stations at once, and keeps running if the internet goes down: tickets keep working and clients can pay cash. Card payments need the internet, so cards wait until it's back.
- Staff sign in with their own Tech ID, and a manager PIN protects sensitive actions.
- The nail salon front desk screen works in English, Vietnamese, Spanish and French.
- A first-time setup walks the owner through sales tax, card payments, cash price, the cash drawer, tip choices, turns and Tech IDs.

## Check-in kiosk
A tablet at the door: clients check in with their phone number or a QR code, choose their service and join the line. It recognizes returning clients and suggests what they usually get, prints a check-in slip, and works in English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Chinese and Korean.

## Walk-in line and waitlist
- Clients can wait from home: they get a text when it is time to head over, based on their drive time and how fast the line is moving.
- When a booked client does not show up, clients on the waitlist get a text.

## Turn rotation
Walk-ins are assigned to technicians by the salon's own rules, with a turn board showing who is next. Six methods: fixed order, equal clients, counted turns, dollar-based turns, dollar brackets, and proportional to price.

## Automatic client texts (Certxa does these on its own)
Booking confirmations, appointment reminders, a rebooking reminder when a client is due for their next visit, win-back texts for clients who have not been back, follow-ups after cancellations and no-shows, and review requests after a visit (once the owner turns review requests on). These automatic texts are included at no extra charge. Marketing text campaigns to a client list use text credits.

## Autumn, the AI receptionist (salon plans)
- Answers the salon's phone, books, reschedules and cancels appointments, answers questions about services, prices and hours, and knows the technicians' schedules.
- While the salon is open she sees who is working and how many people are waiting, and tells callers the real wait.
- After hours she keeps answering and booking.
- Included calls per month by plan (live plan list). Each call beyond that is $0.50, and calls of 30 seconds or less are free and do not count. One-time $15 fee to set up Autumn's phone number.

## Payroll and commissions
Commission rates per staff member (services and add-ons at one rate, retail products at another), tips tracked separately, recurring deductions, pay periods, and printed paychecks that show the tickets behind each commission.

## Loyalty and gift cards
- Loyalty points: clients earn points for every dollar they spend, and the owner sets the rewards (dollars off). Points are redeemed at checkout.
- Gift cards: sold and redeemed at the front desk.

## Reviews and Google
Automatic review requests after visits, all Google reviews in one place, reply from inside Certxa, and the booking link on the Google Business Profile.

## Reports
More than 20 reports: sales, payments, sales tax, tips, gift cards, services, appointments, no-shows, staff, hours worked, turns, wait times, busy times, service times, client retention, clients who haven't been back, top clients, loyalty, reviews, texts and Autumn's calls.

## Website
A website builder for the salon, with its own custom domain.

## What the hardware is for

- Certxa Kiosk: a tablet kiosk where clients check in, see services with photos and prices, pick nail length, shape and design, and join the line. It can also be the customer-facing checkout screen next to a Certxa Station.
- T-Mobile REVVL TAB 2 5G tablet: sold as the customer-facing screen.
- Stripe M2 Card Reader: takes card payments.
- IP Thermal Receipt Printer and Cash Drawer: receipts and cash at the front desk.

## Switching from another system
In Certxa's Data Transfer page the owner uploads the export files from their old system — Vagaro, Square, Fresha, Booksy, GlossGenius, Mindbody, or any CSV or Excel spreadsheet — and Certxa's team imports their clients, services, products, gift cards and appointments.
