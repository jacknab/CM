## Salon operations
### Appointments and online booking
- The salon calendar manages appointments across the team. Owners configure services, staff availability, business hours, booking controls, and the public booking page.
- Clients can choose a service, technician, and available time from a salon's online booking page using a phone or computer. The salon can share its booking link on its own website, Google Business Profile, and Instagram.
- Clients can receive booking confirmations and reminders by text and/or email, and can use the confirmation link to reschedule or cancel, subject to the salon's configured rules.
- Online bookings can require a card on file or a deposit through Stripe when the salon configures that requirement.
- Certxa does not book appointments directly inside Google Search or Maps. Do not promise two-way calendar sync with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, or another provider.

### Clients, services and walk-ins
- Certxa stores salon client records and supports client lookup alongside appointments and front-desk activity.
- Owners manage service catalog entries and related categories, products, add-ons, packages, and deals in the salon app.
- A check-in kiosk lets a client check in with a phone number or QR code, select a service, and join the walk-in line. The kiosk can print a check-in slip.
- Walk-in turn rotation uses the salon's configured rules and a turn board. The six supported methods are fixed order, equal clients, counted turns, dollar-based turns, dollar brackets, and proportional to price.
- Waitlist clients can receive a text when it is time to head over. A booked client's no-show can trigger a text to waitlisted clients.

### Checkout, payments and payroll
- Front-desk checkout supports service/add-on tickets, discounts, tips, split tenders, receipts, cash drawers, and day close. Staff can use individual Tech IDs; a manager PIN protects sensitive actions.
- The front desk can continue handling tickets and cash during an internet outage. Card processing requires internet, except that the Stripe M2 can be configured for Stripe's supported offline-card flow; do not promise that every card transaction works offline.
- Stripe is the integrated card-processing provider. The Stripe M2 reader is the in-person reader. Chip, contactless, and wallet methods (Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Samsung Pay) are listed for the Stripe M2 in current product documentation; supported methods depend on the configured reader/device.
- Public Certxa pricing currently lists Stripe's rate as 2.7% + $0.05 for in-person cards and 2.9% + $0.30 for online cards, plus a $0.60 Certxa fee per card transaction processed through Certxa. These are published rates, not a per-account live quote; do not imply Stripe fees are Certxa's fee or guarantee that Stripe's rate applies to every card/account.
- A salon may use its own external card machine and record the tender in Certxa as a card payment. That is not a Square or other processor integration: Certxa does not process that charge, and the external processor's fees apply.
- No Square payment-processing integration was found. Square is supported as a source format for data transfer/import; do not call that a payment integration.
- Stripe Connect provides the salon's connected payment account and payout information. Payout eligibility, timing, and account requirements depend on Stripe and the connected account; do not promise a payout date or approval.
- Commission settings support staff-specific service/add-on and retail rates, separate tip tracking, recurring deductions, pay periods, and printed checks that show the tickets behind commissions. Do not promise a specific tax, legal, or payroll outcome.
