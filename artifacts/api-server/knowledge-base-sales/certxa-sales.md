# Certxa — facts for the sales line

Plans and prices are loaded live from the pricing page at the start of every call (they follow below this sheet).
Everything here is taken from certxa.com (pricing and features pages, 2026-10-31).

## What Certxa is
All-in-one software for salons: online booking, calendar, front desk checkout (POS), check-in kiosk, waitlist, turn rotation for walk-ins, payroll and commissions, loyalty, automatic client texts, Google reviews, and Autumn, the AI phone receptionist.
Built for nail salons, hair salons, barbershops, spas, massage studios, hair removal studios, and braid and weave salons.

## Free trial and contracts
- Starter, Professional and Enterprise start with a 60-day free trial. No credit card is needed to start.
- Solo is free for one independent professional, with no trial needed.
- Upgrade, downgrade or cancel at any time. No long-term contracts and no commissions.
- Paying yearly costs ten months instead of twelve.
- Sign up at certxa.com.

## Card processing
Payments run through Stripe. In-person card payments are 2.7% + $0.05 and online payments are 2.9% + $0.30 (Stripe's rate), plus a $0.60 Certxa fee per card transaction.

## Autumn, the AI receptionist
Included on the paid plans: 20 calls a month on Starter, 50 on Professional, 100 on Enterprise. Additional calls are $0.50 each, and calls of 30 seconds or less are free. One-time $15 fee to set up Autumn's phone number.
Autumn answers within two seconds, books, reschedules and cancels appointments, answers questions about services and prices, and suggests add-ons. While the salon is open she sees who is working and how many people are waiting, and gives callers a real wait time. After hours she keeps booking.

## Features
- Online booking page: clients pick service, provider and time from any device, from the salon's website, a link, Google or Instagram. Nail options like length, shape and design. Automatic text and e-mail confirmations and reminders, and clients can reschedule from the confirmation link.
- Calendar: live multi-staff calendar, color-coded by team member, drag and drop, block time, print the day's run sheet. Walk-ins show next to bookings.
- Check-in kiosk: a tablet at the door where clients check in with their phone number or a QR code, choose a service and join the line. Recognizes returning clients and suggests what they are due for. Prints a check-in slip. English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Chinese and Korean.
- Front desk checkout (POS): tickets, services, add-ons, discounts, tips, split payments, card reader, cash and gift cards. Receipts, cash drawer, day close with a cash count. Works on several front desk stations and keeps running when the internet goes down.
- Waitlist: walk-ins join a live waitlist, see their estimated wait, and are texted when it is their turn.
- Automatic client texts: confirmations, reminders, waitlist alerts, review requests, and a personal text when a client is due for their next visit. Certxa does this on its own.
- Turn rotation for walk-ins: assigns walk-ins to technicians by the salon's own rules, with a turn board showing who is next.
- Payroll and commissions: commission rates per staff member, tips tracked separately, deductions, pay periods, printed paychecks with the tickets behind each commission.
- Loyalty: a digital punch card or points for every dollar, with rewards the owner sets.
- Growth: Certxa watches bookings and brings back clients who are due, on its own.
- Google reviews: automatic review requests after each visit, all reviews in one place, reply from Certxa.
- The front desk screen for nail salons works in English, Vietnamese, Spanish and French.
