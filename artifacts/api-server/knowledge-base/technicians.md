# Technicians & Turn System

## Overview
Certxa's turn system manages the order in which technicians take clients, ensuring fair and efficient assignment.

## Turn System Modes
- **Simple rotation** — Technicians rotate in a fixed order
- **Revenue-based rotation** — Technicians who have earned less revenue get priority for the next client

## Setting Up the Turn System
1. Go to Settings → Turn System
2. Select your preferred mode
3. Configure any weight/priority adjustments per staff member
4. Save

## Assigning Clients
When a walk-in arrives:
1. Go to Front Desk → Walk-in
2. The system shows who is next in rotation
3. Assign the client to the suggested technician or override manually

## Revenue-Based Turns
In revenue-based mode:
- Each technician's daily revenue is tracked in real time
- The technician with the lowest current revenue goes next
- This ensures income is distributed fairly across all available staff

## Technician Availability
Set each technician's working schedule in Staff → [Member] → Availability. This prevents them from being assigned during off hours.

## Break / Away Status
Technicians can mark themselves as "On Break" or "Away" from the front desk app. They are skipped in rotation while in this status.

## Service Assignments
Each technician can be assigned to specific services. Only technicians who offer a requested service will be included in that service's rotation.

## Tracking Turn History
View full turn history under Reports → Turn Log. Filter by date or technician.

## Troubleshooting
- **Wrong technician suggested**: Check if the availability and service assignments are correct
- **Revenue not updating**: Ensure appointments are being marked "Completed" after service
