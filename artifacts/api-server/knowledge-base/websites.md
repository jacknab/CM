# Salon Websites

## Overview
Certxa includes a website builder that lets you create a professional business website with your brand, services, and a built-in booking button.

## Creating Your Website
1. Go to Website Builder in the main menu
2. Choose a template
3. Customize colors, fonts, logo, and content
4. Add your services, team bios, and photos
5. Connect your booking page
6. Publish

## Custom Domain
You can connect a custom domain (e.g., mysalon.com) to your Certxa website:
1. Go to Website → Settings → Domain
2. Enter your custom domain
3. Follow the DNS instructions provided
4. DNS propagation can take up to 48 hours

## Editing Your Website
- Open Website Builder and click "Edit"
- Changes are saved as drafts until you click "Publish"
- Published changes go live immediately

## Website Sections
- **Hero** — Headline and call-to-action (Book Now button)
- **Services** — Your service menu pulled from Certxa
- **Team** — Staff profiles and photos
- **Gallery** — Photos from your business
- **Reviews** — Google review feed (requires Google Business connection)
- **Contact** — Phone, address, hours, map

## SEO Settings
Configure your page title, meta description, and social share image under Website → SEO Settings.

## Website Analytics
Basic traffic stats (page views, booking button clicks) are available under Website → Analytics.

## Troubleshooting
- **Website not loading**: Check if it's published under Website → Settings → Status
- **Domain not working**: Verify DNS records are configured correctly
- **Booking button broken**: Ensure online booking is enabled in Settings → Booking Settings
- **Changes not showing**: Make sure you clicked "Publish" after editing
