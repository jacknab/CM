# Point of Sale (POS)

## Overview
Certxa's POS system lets you process payments, track transactions, apply discounts, and handle gift cards — all from your dashboard.

## Processing a Payment
1. Go to Front Desk or open a completed appointment
2. Click "Check Out"
3. Review the services and prices
4. Apply any discounts, gift cards, or loyalty points
5. Select payment method: Cash, Card, or Other
6. Complete the transaction

## Payment Methods
- **Credit/Debit Card** — Processed via Stripe (requires Stripe setup)
- **Cash** — Log cash payments manually
- **Gift Card** — Apply a gift card by code
- **Split Payment** — Combine multiple payment methods
- **Loyalty Points** — Redeem points toward the total

## Applying Discounts
- Fixed dollar amount discount
- Percentage discount
- Promo codes (if configured)

## Refunds
- Open the transaction from Sales History
- Click "Refund"
- Select full or partial refund amount
- For card payments, the refund goes back to the original card (3–5 business days)

## Receipts
Receipts are sent automatically via SMS or email if configured. You can also print receipts if a receipt printer is connected.

## Tips
- Staff can accept tips during checkout
- Tips are tracked per staff member for payroll purposes

## Sales History
View all transactions under Reports → Sales. Filter by date, staff, service, or payment method.

## Cash Drawer
If using a connected cash drawer, it opens automatically on cash sales. Manual open option available for end-of-day reconciliation.
