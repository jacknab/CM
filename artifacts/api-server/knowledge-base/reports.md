# Reports & Analytics

## Overview
Certxa provides comprehensive reporting to help you understand your business performance.

## Available Reports

### Revenue Report
- Total revenue by day, week, month, or year
- Revenue by service
- Revenue by staff member
- Comparison to previous periods

### Appointment Report
- Total appointments booked
- Completion rate vs. no-shows vs. cancellations
- Busiest days and times
- Booking source (online vs. walk-in vs. phone)

### Client Report
- New clients acquired
- Returning client rate
- Average visits per client
- Client lifetime value

### Staff Report
- Appointments per staff member
- Revenue per staff member
- Average ticket value per staff

### Payroll Report
- Hours worked per staff
- Commission earned
- Tips received
- Total pay owed

### Inventory Report (if enabled)
- Product sales
- Low stock alerts

## Filtering Reports
All reports can be filtered by:
- Date range (custom, weekly, monthly, yearly)
- Location (for multi-location businesses)
- Staff member
- Service

## Exporting Reports
Export any report to CSV or PDF from the report page.

## Scheduled Reports
Set up automatic weekly or monthly report emails under Settings → Reports → Scheduled Reports.

## Dashboard Overview
The main dashboard shows a real-time summary:
- Today's appointments and revenue
- Fill rate (booked vs. available slots)
- Recent bookings
- Staff utilization

## Common Questions
- **Revenue includes tax?** — Configurable in Settings → Reports → Tax Settings
- **Reports seem off?** — Verify appointment statuses are updated (Completed vs. Pending)
