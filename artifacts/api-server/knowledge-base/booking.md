# Online Booking

## Overview
Certxa provides a public online booking page that your clients can use 24/7 to book appointments without calling.

## Setting Up Online Booking
1. Go to Settings → Booking Settings
2. Enable "Online Booking"
3. Your booking page URL is: `certxa.com/book/[your-slug]`
4. Share this link on your website, social media, or Google Business profile

## Booking Slug
Your booking slug is your unique URL identifier. It can be customized in Settings → Business Info.

## Booking Rules
- **Advance booking window** — How far in advance clients can book (e.g., 30 days)
- **Minimum notice** — Minimum hours before a slot that a client can book online (e.g., 2 hours)
- **Same-day booking** — Can be enabled or disabled in booking settings
- **Buffer time** — Automatic gap between appointments

## Service Visibility
Control which services appear on your public booking page in Settings → Services. Toggle "Show on Booking Page" for each service.

## Staff Selection
Clients can optionally select a preferred staff member. If no preference, Certxa assigns based on availability.

## Booking Confirmations
Clients receive an automatic confirmation email/SMS when they book online (if notifications are configured).

## Approval Mode
Enable "Booking Approval Required" if you want to manually approve each online booking before it's confirmed.

## Deposit Requirements
Certxa supports requiring a deposit for online bookings. Configure the deposit amount in Settings → Booking Settings.

## Troubleshooting Online Booking
- **Page not found**: Check your booking slug is set and online booking is enabled
- **No slots available**: Check business hours, staff availability, and service duration
- **Client can't book**: Check minimum notice settings and advance booking window
