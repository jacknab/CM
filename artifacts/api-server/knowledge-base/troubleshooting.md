# Troubleshooting

## Login Issues
- **Can't log in**: Reset your password at the login page using "Forgot Password"
- **2FA code not working**: Ensure your phone clock is correct. Try resending the code.
- **Account locked**: Contact support — accounts lock after 10 failed login attempts

## Booking System Issues
- **Appointments not showing**: Check if you're on the correct date in the calendar view
- **Can't create appointment**: Verify staff availability and business hours are configured
- **Online booking not working**: Check that the booking slug is set and online booking is enabled
- **Slots showing as unavailable**: Confirm business hours, staff schedule, and service duration settings

## Payment Issues
- **Card payment failing**: Verify Stripe is connected and your Stripe account is active
- **POS not loading**: Try a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). Clear browser cache if needed.
- **Can't process refund**: Refunds must be within 90 days of the original transaction

## Staff Issues
- **Staff not appearing in scheduling**: Check staff is set as Active in their profile
- **Staff can't clock in**: Ensure they have the correct role and PIN configured
- **Commission not calculating**: Appointment must be in "Completed" status

## Notification Issues
- **SMS not sending**: Verify your SMS balance under Settings → Billing → Wallet
- **Email not delivering**: Check the client's email is correct and not marked as invalid
- **Notifications not triggering**: Go to Settings → Notifications and verify templates are enabled

## Website Issues
- **Website offline**: Check publish status under Website → Settings
- **Domain not resolving**: Verify DNS records. Allow up to 48 hours for propagation.
- **Booking button not working**: Ensure online booking is enabled

## App Performance
- **Slow loading**: Try a different browser. Check your internet connection.
- **Data not syncing**: Log out and back in. Contact support if issue persists.
- **Browser not supported**: Certxa works best in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (latest versions)

## Emergency Issues
If you experience any of the following, contact support immediately and this will be marked high priority:
- System completely down
- Cannot process any payments
- All appointments disappeared
- Client data missing
- Payroll data incorrect
- Website offline for more than 30 minutes
