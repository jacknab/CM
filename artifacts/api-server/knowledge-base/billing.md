# Billing & Account

## Overview
Certxa is a subscription-based SaaS platform. Your account is billed monthly or annually based on your selected plan.

## Viewing Your Bill
- Go to Settings → Billing
- View your current plan, next billing date, and invoice history
- Download PDF invoices for any past charge

## Changing Your Plan
- Go to Settings → Billing → Change Plan
- Select the new plan and confirm
- Upgrades take effect immediately (prorated)
- Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle

## Payment Method
- Update your credit card under Settings → Billing → Payment Method
- Certxa uses Stripe for all billing — your card is never stored on Certxa servers

## Failed Payments
If a payment fails:
1. You'll receive an email notification
2. Certxa will retry the charge automatically (3 attempts over 7 days)
3. If all retries fail, your account will enter a grace period
4. Update your payment method to restore full access

## Cancelling Your Subscription
- Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Plan
- Your account remains active until the end of the current billing period
- All your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation before deletion

## Refund Policy
Certxa does not offer prorated refunds for partial months. If you have a billing concern, contact support and we'll review your case.

## Tax / VAT
Certxa collects applicable sales tax based on your business address. VAT invoices are available for international customers.

## Adding a Wallet Balance
Some features (AI Receptionist, SMS credits) are billed from a prepaid wallet balance:
- Go to Settings → Billing → Add Funds
- Select an amount and pay via card
- Funds are added immediately

## Common Billing Issues
- **Unexpected charge**: Check your invoice history for details. Contact support if unrecognized.
- **Card declined**: Update your payment method or contact your bank
- **Can't access account after payment**: Contact support — we can manually restore access
