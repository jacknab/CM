# Stripe Integration

## Overview
Certxa uses Stripe to process client payments, manage subscriptions, and handle payouts to contractors.

## Connecting Stripe
1. Go to Settings → Payments → Connect Stripe
2. Click "Connect with Stripe"
3. Sign in to your Stripe account (or create one)
4. Complete the onboarding — Stripe will verify your business details
5. Once connected, card payments are enabled in your POS

## Stripe Account Types
- **Standard Connect** — Certxa processes payments on behalf of your Stripe account
- **Express Connect** — Your business receives payouts directly to your bank

## Accepting Card Payments
Once Stripe is connected, card payments appear as an option during checkout. Clients can also save their card for online bookings.

## Payouts
Stripe deposits funds to your connected bank account on your payout schedule (typically 2 business days). Configure payout frequency in your Stripe dashboard.

## Stripe Fees
Stripe charges a processing fee per transaction (typically 2.9% + 30¢). This is separate from your Certxa subscription.

## Refunds via Stripe
Refunds initiated in Certxa are automatically processed through Stripe and returned to the original card (3–5 business days).

## Stripe Dashboard
For detailed transaction history, disputes, and payout reports, log in directly to dashboard.stripe.com.

## Disconnecting Stripe
Go to Settings → Payments → Disconnect Stripe. This disables card payments until reconnected. Pending payouts are not affected.

## Troubleshooting Stripe
- **Payment declined**: The client's bank declined the charge. Ask them to use a different card.
- **Card not processing**: Verify Stripe is connected and your Stripe account is in good standing.
- **Payouts delayed**: Check your Stripe dashboard for any verification requirements or holds.
- **Dispute/chargeback**: Respond via your Stripe dashboard. Contact support if you need help.
