# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is Certxa?**
Certxa is an all-in-one salon and service business management platform. It handles bookings, front desk operations, POS, payroll, client management, loyalty rewards, and more.

**What devices does Certxa work on?**
Certxa works in any modern web browser on desktop, tablet, or mobile. No app download is required, though a mobile-optimized interface is available.

**Can I use Certxa for multiple locations?**
Yes. Multi-location is supported on Professional and Enterprise plans. Each location has its own staff, services, and calendar.

**Is there a mobile app?**
Certxa has a mobile-optimized web interface. A dedicated iOS/Android app is on the roadmap.

**How do I get started?**
Sign up at certxa.com, complete the onboarding steps, add your services and staff, and you're ready to take bookings.

## Pricing & Billing

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes — new accounts get a 60-day free trial with full Professional features. No credit card required.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Cancel from Settings → Billing. Your account remains active until the end of the billing period.

**Do you offer refunds?**
Certxa does not issue prorated refunds for partial months. Contact support for billing concerns and we'll review case by case.

## Features

**Does Certxa send appointment reminders?**
Yes. Automated SMS and email reminders can be configured under Settings → Notifications.

**Can clients book online?**
Yes. Your unique online booking page is available 24/7 at certxa.com/book/[your-slug].

**Does Certxa have a loyalty program?**
Yes. Points are awarded per dollar spent and can be redeemed for discounts. Configure under Settings → Loyalty.

**Can I track inventory?**
Basic product sales tracking is included. Advanced inventory with low-stock alerts is on the roadmap.

**Does Certxa integrate with Google?**
Yes. Connect your Google Business Profile to sync reviews and business hours. Google OAuth login is also supported.

## Technical

**Is my data secure?**
Yes. Certxa uses bank-level encryption, secure HTTPS connections, and daily database backups. Payment data is handled entirely by Stripe.

**Can I export my data?**
Yes. Export clients, appointments, and reports as CSV files from the respective pages.

**What happens to my data if I cancel?**
Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation, then permanently deleted. Export your data before cancelling.
