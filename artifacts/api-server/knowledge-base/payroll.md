# Payroll

## Overview
Certxa tracks staff hours and generates payroll reports based on clock-in/out records, commissions, and tips.

## Staff Hours
Staff clock in and out using the Certxa app or tablet at the front desk. Hours are automatically tracked.

## Viewing Payroll
Go to Reports → Payroll to see:
- Total hours per staff member
- Service revenue generated
- Commission earned
- Tips received
- Total pay owed

## Pay Periods
Configure your pay period in Settings → Payroll:
- Weekly
- Bi-weekly
- Semi-monthly
- Monthly

## Commission Tracking
Each staff member can have a commission rate set in their profile:
- **Flat rate** — e.g., $X per appointment
- **Percentage** — e.g., 40% of service revenue
- **Tiered** — different rates at different revenue thresholds

## Tips
Tips collected through the POS are automatically assigned to the staff member who performed the service.

## Exporting Payroll
Export payroll data to CSV for import into your accounting software (QuickBooks, Gusto, etc.) from Reports → Payroll → Export.

## Overtime
Certxa automatically flags hours over 40/week (or your configured threshold) as overtime in the payroll report.

## Troubleshooting Payroll Issues
- **Missing hours**: Check if the staff member clocked in/out properly
- **Wrong commission rate**: Verify the rate in Staff → [Member] → Commission Settings
- **Tips not showing**: Ensure tips were entered during checkout
