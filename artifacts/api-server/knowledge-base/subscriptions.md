# Subscriptions & Plans

## Overview
Certxa offers multiple subscription tiers designed for different business sizes.

## Available Plans

### Starter
- 1 location
- Up to 3 staff members
- Online booking
- Basic reports
- Email support

### Professional
- Up to 3 locations
- Unlimited staff
- AI Receptionist (add-on)
- Advanced reports
- Payroll tracking
- Priority support

### Enterprise
- Unlimited locations
- All features included
- Custom integrations
- Dedicated account manager
- SLA guarantee

## Viewing Your Current Plan
Go to Settings → Billing to see your active plan, features included, and usage limits.

## Upgrading
- Upgrades take effect immediately
- You are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing cycle

## Downgrading
- Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle
- If you exceed limits of the lower plan, you'll be prompted to upgrade again

## Feature Limits
Each plan has limits on:
- Number of locations
- Number of staff members
- SMS credits per month
- Storage for photos/files

## Free Trial
New accounts receive a 60-day free trial with full Professional features. No credit card required to start.

## Trial Expiration
When your trial expires, you'll be prompted to select a plan. If no plan is selected within the grace period, access to most features is restricted until billing is set up.

## Annual Billing
Switch to annual billing for a discount (typically 2 months free). Contact support to switch billing frequency.
