# Commissions

## Overview
Certxa tracks service commissions for each staff member automatically based on completed appointments.

## Setting Up Commission Rates
1. Go to Staff → Select staff member → Edit
2. Scroll to "Commission Settings"
3. Choose commission type and rate
4. Save

## Commission Types
- **Percentage of service revenue** — e.g., 45% of each service they perform
- **Flat fee per service** — e.g., $15 per appointment
- **Tiered commission** — Higher rate after hitting a revenue milestone (e.g., 40% up to $2,000, then 50%)
- **Service-specific rates** — Different commission rates per service type

## Viewing Commission Reports
Go to Reports → Commissions to see:
- Commission earned per staff member
- Breakdown by service
- Comparison across time periods

## Commission vs. Wage
Certxa lets you set both an hourly wage and a commission rate per staff member. If a staff member earns more from commission than their wage, commission takes precedence (configurable in settings).

## Contractor vs. Employee
- **Employees** — Hourly or salary-based, commissions tracked alongside wages
- **Contractors** — Commission or flat-rate only, tracked separately for 1099 purposes

## Adjustments
If a commission needs to be manually adjusted (e.g., for a refund), go to Reports → Commissions → Adjust.

## Common Issues
- **Commission not calculating**: Ensure the appointment status is "Completed"
- **Wrong rate**: Double-check the commission settings on the staff member's profile
- **Tiered not triggering**: Verify the revenue threshold period (weekly vs. monthly)
