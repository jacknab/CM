Frequently Asked Questions

What types of salons does Certxa work for?

Certxa works for nail salons, hair salons, beauty studios, lash studios, waxing studios, and any appointment-based salon business. Whether you're a solo stylist or running a multi-chair salon with multiple staff, Certxa scales with you.

Can clients book specific stylists or technicians?

Yes — clients can browse your team, see each person's availability, and book the specific stylist or technician they want. Each staff member has their own schedule, services, and real-time availability shown to clients.

How do automated reminders work?

Certxa automatically sends SMS and email reminders before every appointment. You control the timing — 24 hours before, 2 hours before, or both. Reminders reduce no-shows by an average of 40% without any manual work.

Can I take deposits or payments online at booking?

Yes. You can require a deposit to hold the booking slot, or collect full payment upfront. All payments process securely online and are tracked automatically in your POS history.
